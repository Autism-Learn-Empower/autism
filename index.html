About us

Learning by doing, every step of the way.

Autism Learn & Empower Pakistan works directly with children with autism and developmental delays on the everyday skills that matter most — fine and gross motor coordination, focus and attention, following instructions, colour and shape recognition, independence, and social interaction.

We don't believe in one-size-fits-all therapy. Every session is built around simple, repeatable activities — reaching, matching, threading, stacking — that quietly build cognitive, motor, and sensory skills at the child's own pace, with parents and educators involved every step of the way.

We are not a charity or nonprofit — we are a practical, fee-based service. But we also believe support shouldn't depend on income, so fees are adjusted to what each family can genuinely afford.

Our approach

Why simple activities work

Every session is built around real, filmed, analysed activities — not guesswork. Here's what a typical activity actually builds:

01

Pick a simple, repeatable activity

Colour matching, ring threading, reaching, sorting — activities the child can repeat comfortably and confidently.

02

Layer in real skills

Each repetition quietly builds fine/gross motor control, hand-eye coordination, focus, and instruction-following.

03

Support, don't rush

Gentle prompts, gestures, and repetition — never pressure. The child sets the pace.

04

Track real progress

We watch for independence, persistence, and confidence growing session over session.
